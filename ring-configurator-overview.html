How it works

Five clear choices. One configured ring.

The experience keeps the ring visible while shoppers move from design decisions to a complete demo configuration.

  1. 01

    Preview the stone fit

    Choose Preview Stone type, origin, shape, target carat, and diamond-only cut/clarity controls.

  2. 02

    Pick the setting/head

    Select a compatible head or setting style for the ring setting preview.

  3. 03

    Configure the band

    Choose ring style, ring size, and supported matching band options.

  4. 04

    Choose metal and engraving

    Set metal color, purity/karat, optional bi-metal head accent, and optional inscription.

  5. 05

    Review and continue

    Check the grouped build summary and Preview Total, then continue to the Choose Diamond step.

Helpful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for shoppers as they explore, personalize, and review their ring setting.

How does the Ring Configurator improve the shopping experience?

It lets shoppers build confidence before moving forward by showing ring setting options, preview stone preferences, engraving, and pricing details in one interactive 3D experience.

Why is a preview stone shown before diamond selection?

The preview stone helps shoppers visualize proportion, fit, and overall design balance. The final center diamond is selected separately in the next step.

Can shoppers compare lab-grown and natural diamond preferences?

Yes. Shoppers can preview lab-grown or natural diamond preferences to understand how the design and preview pricing change before choosing the final diamond.

What parts of the ring can be personalized?

Shoppers can configure the setting style, head style, metal color, purity, ring size, matching band, bi-metal accent, engraving, and supported preview stone options.

How does AI guidance help during configuration?

The AI Assistant helps shoppers narrow choices, apply supported options, compare preferences, and make quicker design decisions without leaving the configurator.

What does the build summary show?

The summary gives shoppers a clear review of the selected setting, head, preview stone, engraving, and pricing details before they continue to diamond selection.

Can shoppers share their configured design?

Yes. The Share action lets shoppers copy or share the current design so they can review it later or discuss it before continuing.

What happens after the ring setting is configured?

After reviewing the configured setting, shoppers can continue to the diamond selection step where the final center stone is chosen.
